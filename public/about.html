About La Pizzariô

Crafted with passion. Baked to perfection.

La Pizzariô began with one simple belief — that great pizza should never be a luxury. From our wood-fired ovens across Bengal, we serve hot, fresh and mouth-watering pizzas at unbelievable prices, made the way pizza should be: generous with cheese, honest with ingredients, and always fresh to order.

Every base is hand-stretched, every topping is prepared daily, and every pizza leaves our kitchen only when it meets one standard — would we serve this to our own family?

Experience the finest flavors, only at La Pizzariô.
